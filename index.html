AI for a Sustainable Future

Sustainability Lab · IIT Gandhinagar

We build AI that serves both people and the planet — from air quality and health sensing to energy and Earth observation.

The lab is led by Nipun Batra. Our alumni have gone on to Cambridge, Microsoft Research, Google DeepMind, CMU and industry.

Recent recognition: Best Paper Runner-Up at BuildSys 2026, the ACM e-Energy Test-of-Time Award 2025 for NILMTK, and the ACM SIGEnergy Rising Star 2025. Work published at NeurIPS, KDD, AAAI, IMWUT, BuildSys and UbiComp.
